Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

At Global Opportunity Hub, we value your privacy. We do not require visitors to register an account or provide personal identification to browse opportunities on our platform.

  • Newsletter Subscription: If you voluntarily subscribe to our weekly newsletter, we collect your email address solely to send opportunity alerts. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any email.
  • Local Bookmarks: Your saved opportunities are stored purely in your browser's local storage (`localStorage`). This data never leaves your personal device.
  • Analytics & Usage: We may use anonymous analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to aggregate page views, referrer domains, and device types to improve site speed and user experience.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Cookies

We may display third-party advertisements served by networks such as Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings (www.google.com/settings/ads) or through the Network Advertising Initiative.

3. Contact Information

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please email our privacy team at: contact@globalopportunityhub.com.
