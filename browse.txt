## Overview
The World Bank Winter Internship Program invites graduate students from across the globe to bring fresh perspectives, innovative ideas, and research experience into the World Bank's global operations. Most positions are based at World Bank headquarters in Washington, D.C., with select opportunities across country offices worldwide.

## Compensation & Benefits
- **Hourly Salary**: Competitive hourly wage paid to all interns in accordance with World Bank operational scales.
- **Travel Allowance**: Up to $3,000 for round-trip economy airfare to the duty station (discretionary based on hiring manager approval).
- **Direct Mentorship**: Daily collaboration with world-leading economists, environmental scientists, and financial analysts.
- **Global Network**: Connect with fellow international interns and access the World Bank knowledge repositories.

## Eligibility Criteria
- **Enrollment Status**: Must hold an undergraduate degree and be currently enrolled in a full-time graduate study program (pursuing a Master's degree or PhD) with plans to return to school.
- **Fields of Study**: Economics, Finance, Human Development (Public Health, Education, Nutrition), Social Sciences, Agriculture, Environment, Engineering, Urban Planning, or related disciplines.
- **Languages**: Fluency in English is required. Knowledge of other languages such as French, Spanish, Russian, Arabic, or Portuguese is an asset.
- **Technical Skills**: Computing skills, econometric modeling, and data science capabilities are highly advantageous.

## Required Documents
- Curriculum Vitae (CV) in PDF format.
- Statement of Interest (maximum 1 page).
- Official proof of current enrollment in an accredited Master’s or PhD program.

## How to Apply (Step-by-Step)
1. **Prepare Profile**: Review eligibility and update your CV and Statement of Interest before the deadline.
2. **Access World Bank Member Center**: Submit your application through the official Taleo recruitment portal.
3. **Application Review**: Candidates are shortlisted by departments based on operational project needs for the December–March period.

## Overview
The Harvard Radcliffe Institute Fellowship Program brings together scholars, artists, scientists, and practitioners from around the globe to pursue transformative work free from customary obligations. Fellows step away from routine duties to immerse themselves in deeply focused research or artistic creation within Harvard University’s vibrant intellectual community.

## Fellowship Benefits
- **Substantial Stipend**: $78,000 for the nine-month academic year, plus an additional $5,000 project allowance.
- **Housing Assistance**: Access to Harvard University housing assistance and relocation support.
- **Dedicated Office/Studio**: Private study or creative studio located at the Radcliffe Yard in Cambridge, MA.
- **Harvard Library Access**: Full borrowing privileges and digital access to Harvard University’s library system (the largest academic library in the world).
- **Health Insurance**: Relocation healthcare subsidy and eligibility to enroll in Harvard University group health plans.

## Eligibility Criteria
- **Academic Applicants**: Must hold a doctorate (PhD, MD, or terminal degree) earned at least two years prior to the appointment year, with significant published scholarship.
- **Creative Writers, Artists, and Journalists**: Evaluated on professional achievements, portfolio strength, and public recognition in their creative discipline.
- **Commitment**: Fellows must reside in the Greater Boston area and participate actively in the Radcliffe community throughout the academic fellowship year (September through May).

## Required Documents
- Online application form.
- Curriculum Vitae (CV) including complete list of publications or creative exhibitions.
- Project proposal (max 1,400 words) describing project goals, methodology, and significance.
- Writing or artistic work sample.
- Three letters of recommendation submitted directly by referees.

## How to Apply (Step-by-Step)
1. **Access Radcliffe Portal**: Create an applicant account on Harvard Radcliffe’s official fellowship submission portal.
2. **Designate Referees**: Enter contact details for three professional referees early so they have sufficient time to submit recommendations.
3. **Upload Proposal**: Upload your project proposal, CV, and creative/scholarly work sample before the stated deadline.
4. **Final Review**: Applications are reviewed through a rigorous two-tier peer-review committee process.

## Overview
The Junior Professional Officer (JPO) Programme provides young professionals an exceptional opportunity to acquire hands-on experience in multilateral international development cooperation and humanitarian policy under the guidance of senior staff at UNICEF.

JPOs are assigned to UNICEF field offices across Africa, Asia, Latin America, or headquarters in New York, Geneva, and Copenhagen, serving vulnerable children and families worldwide.

## Compensation & Benefits
- **United Nations P-2 Salary Scale**: Annual base tax-exempt salary starting from $75,000 to $92,000 plus cost-of-living post adjustment.
- **Relocation & Travel**: Full travel costs, shipping allowance, and relocation grant for the candidate and recognized dependents.
- **Healthcare & Pension**: Comprehensive worldwide health, dental, and life insurance, plus participation in the United Nations Joint Staff Pension Fund (UNJSPF).
- **Annual Leave**: 30 working days of annual paid leave per year plus official UN holidays.
- **Career Mentorship**: Dedicated professional development courses, language learning grants, and career coaching.

## Eligibility Criteria
- **Education**: Advanced university degree (Master's or equivalent) in Social Sciences, International Relations, Public Health, Education, Economics, or Law.
- **Experience**: Minimum of 2 years of relevant professional experience at the national or international level.
- **Age**: Under 32 or 35 years of age on 31 December of the application year (varies by donor government agreement).
- **Language**: Fluency in English (working knowledge of French, Spanish, Arabic, or Russian is an advantage).

## Required Documents
- Completed UN P11 Personal History Profile / Online e-Recruitment Profile.
- Copy of highest educational diploma and certified academic transcripts.
- Detailed CV highlighting relevant child rights or humanitarian work.
- Two professional references from previous employers or academic advisors.

## How to Apply (Step-by-Step)
1. **Visit UNICEF Careers**: Navigate to the official UNICEF recruitment portal and filter for Junior Professional Officer vacancies.
2. **Review Nationality Eligibility**: Confirm whether your country has a bilateral funding agreement with the UN for JPO positions.
3. **Submit Online Profile**: Fill out the official UN application, upload documents, and write a motivational statement.
4. **Competency Assessment**: Shortlisted applicants will undergo technical written assessments and UN competency-based panel interviews.

## Overview
Each year the Swiss Confederation awards Government Excellence Scholarships to promote international exchange and research cooperation between Switzerland and over 180 other countries. The research scholarship is available to post-graduate researchers in any discipline who hold a master’s degree or PhD, intending to pursue doctoral or post-doctoral research in Switzerland.

The scholarships are awarded by the Federal Commission for Scholarships for Foreign Students (FCS) in collaboration with Swiss public universities, federal institutes of technology (ETH Zurich, EPFL), and universities of applied sciences.

## Program Levels & Duration
- **PhD Scholarships**: 12 months, renewable up to 36 months based on achieved academic results.
- **Research Fellowships**: Up to 12 months maximum (intended for highly qualified doctoral or medical students as well as postgraduates wishing to conduct research).
- **Postdoctoral Scholarships**: 12 months.

## Benefits & Financial Coverage
- **Monthly Stipend**: CHF 2,450 per month (approx. $2,800 USD) for PhD and Research Fellowship candidates.
- **Health Insurance**: Mandatory Swiss National Health Insurance is fully covered by the FCS for non-EU/EFTA scholars.
- **Housing Allowance**: One-time lump sum grant of CHF 600 paid upon arrival to assist with apartment rental deposits.
- **Public Transportation**: A Swiss public transport Half-Fare Travelcard (valid for 1 year) is provided free of charge.
- **Flight Allowance**: Return flight allowance / lump sum payment provided at the end of the scholarship for candidates from non-EU/EFTA countries.
- **Support Services**: Dedicated trips, cultural dinners, and sightseeing tours organized for Swiss Government Excellence Scholars.

## Important Limitations
> **Note**: Tuition and semester registration fees are **not** covered by the FCS scholarship. Most Swiss public universities waive or reduce these fees for FCS scholars, but applicants must confirm with their host university. Applicants must apply to **only one host university**.

## Eligibility Criteria
- **Academic Degree**: Master's degree or equivalent university degree achieved before 31 July 2027, with high academic standing.
- **Age Limit**: Born after 31 December 1991 (must not be older than 35 at the start of the scholarship).
- **Host Professor Support**: Mandatory letter of support from an academic supervisor (professor) at a recognized Swiss university willing to supervise the applicant's research.
- **Research Proposal**: A detailed, independent research proposal (max 5 pages) complete with a feasible timeline.
- **Language Proficiency**: Excellent proficiency in the language of instruction (English, French, German, or Italian) required for the selected research program.

## Required Documents
1. Official FCS Application Form (duly signed with photograph).
2. Comprehensive Curriculum Vitae (CV) listing academic publications and awards.
3. Letter of Motivation (maximum 2 pages).
4. Complete Research Proposal (up to 5 pages) using the official FCS template.
5. Written confirmation from a professor at a Swiss institution agreeing to supervise the research.
6. Short CV of the supervising professor.
7. Two letters of recommendation from academic referees on official FCS recommendation forms.
8. Certified photocopies of university diplomas, transcripts, and grading scales.
9. Medical certificate on official FCS form.
10. Two copies of the applicant's valid passport.

## How to Apply (Step-by-Step)
1. **Identify a Swiss Supervisor**: Reach out to professors at Swiss universities (ETH Zurich, EPFL, University of Geneva, University of Zurich, University of Basel, etc.) matching your research domain and secure their written commitment.
2. **Obtain Application Package**: Contact the Swiss Embassy in your home country (e.g., Swiss Embassy in Islamabad for Pakistan, or your national diplomatic mission) to obtain the official FCS application documents and country-specific fact sheet.
3. **Prepare Dossier**: Complete the FCS form and compile three hard-copy sets (1 original + 2 copies) of all certified documents.
4. **Submit to Swiss Embassy**: Submit the full paper dossier directly to the Swiss Embassy in your home country before the local deadline.
   - **Deadline for Pakistan**: 31 October 2026 (11:59 PM Swiss time).
   - *Note: Deadlines vary between August and December depending on each country's Swiss embassy.*
5. **Selection & Announcement**: The Swiss Embassy conducts preliminary screening, and FCS makes the final decision by May 2027.

## Overview
Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST), renowned as the leading science and technology research university in South Korea, invites international students for full-time graduate degree admissions. All admitted international students who maintain satisfactory academic standing are eligible for the prestigious KAIST International Student Scholarship.

## Scholarship Benefits
- **Full Tuition Exemption**: 100% waiver of tuition fees throughout the normative duration of study (4 semesters for Master's, 8 semesters for PhD).
- **Monthly Living Stipend**: KRW 350,000 per month for Master's students and KRW 400,000 per month for Doctoral students.
- **Research Project Grants**: Additional research assistantship stipends provided by individual laboratory advisors (average KRW 800,000–1,500,000/month).
- **National Health Insurance**: Subsidized health insurance coverage.
- **On-Campus Housing**: Guaranteed university dormitory options at subsidized rates.

## Eligibility Criteria
- **Nationality**: Candidates who hold non-Korean nationality (neither the applicant nor applicant's parents may hold Korean citizenship).
- **Academic Degree**:
  - For Master's: Bachelor’s degree from an accredited institution by February 2027.
  - For PhD: Master’s degree from an accredited institution by February 2027.
- **English Proficiency**: Official score report meeting one of: TOEFL iBT 83, IELTS 6.5, TOEIC 720, or TEPS 326 (scores must be within 2 years of the deadline).

## Required Documents
- KAIST Online Application Form and Document Checklist.
- Statement of Purpose (SOP) and Study Plan.
- Two Recommendation Letters from academic professors.
- Official Degree Certificates and University Transcripts (with apostille or consular authentication).
- English Proficiency Test Score.
- Passport copy and proof of parent-child relationship.

## How to Apply (Step-by-Step)
1. **Online Application**: Register on the KAIST Graduate Admissions Portal, fill out all application sections, and pay the 80 USD application fee.
2. **Recommendation Letters**: Your recommenders must submit their evaluation letters through the automated online recommendation system.
3. **Send Physical Documents**: Mail your certified physical documents to the KAIST Admissions Office in Daejeon, South Korea.
4. **Admissions Result**: Check admissions and scholarship results announced on the portal.

- **Health Coverage**: Accident and sickness health benefits that meet J-1 visa guidelines.
- **Community Seed Grants**: Access to competitive small grant funding (up to $5,000) to execute your Community Action Project upon returning home.
- **Leadership Institute**: Tailored curriculum designed by top leadership scholars with executive coaching.

4. **Women and Gender Issues**: Gender-based violence prevention, women's economic empowerment.

- **Experience**: Minimum of two years of active experience working on community development (as a professional or volunteer).

2. **Essay Prompts**: Submit detailed responses explaining your community experience and your proposed Community Action Project.
3. **Reference Verification**: Provide contact details for your professional supervisor.
4. **Semifinalist Interview**: Semifinalists participate in an online interview and submit English language verification scores.
